By air

Maharana Pratap Airport (Dabok) is about 22 km away, roughly 40 minutes by road. Cars will meet every family flight.

By train

Udaipur City station is about 15 minutes from the haveli. Tell us your train and someone will be on the platform.

Stay

Rooms are held at the haveli and two lakeside hotels nearby. Write to the help desk for your room details.

Parking

Valet at the main gate. Overflow parking at the lakeside lot, with a shuttle every 15 minutes.
